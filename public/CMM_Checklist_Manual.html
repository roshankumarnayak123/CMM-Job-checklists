📋 CMM Checklist - User Manual

Welcome to the official Central Mechanical Maintenance (CMM) Checklist application manual. This platform digitizes and tracks maintenance protocols across departments, ensuring compliance and easy reporting.

1. Overview

The CMM Checklist application consists of two main perspectives:

  • Technician View: Fill out assigned maintenance checklists, attach photo evidence, and sign off on completion.
  • Admin Dashboard: Create checklist templates, review submitted records, and monitor cloud usage.

2. For Technicians (Filling Checklists)

  • Selecting a Task: Use the left-hand sidebar to select the specific maintenance task/checklist assigned to you.
  • Data Entry: Provide your Name, Designation, and Date. Follow the instructions for each checkpoint. Input types range from simple Yes/No toggles to specific number readings.
  • Photo Evidence: For critical steps, click "Attach Photo" to capture and upload an image of the machinery or reading.
  • Submission: Once complete, click "Submit Checklist". You will receive a Tracking Code upon successful upload which can be used for future reference.

3. For Administrators

  • Dashboard: Upon logging in, you will see recent submissions across all departments on your main dashboard.
  • Template Builder: Click "Create New Checklist" in the sidebar to build a new template. You can add required or optional checkpoints.
  • Reviewing Submissions: Click on any completed submission card to view the technician's notes, answers, and uploaded photos in detail.
  • Settings & Management: Access cloud usage statistics, manage your account password, and view this manual via the Settings button at the top right.
Note: Ensure you have a stable internet connection when submitting checklists, especially if they contain multiple high-resolution photos.
